Profile

Data platform engineer and architect with 19 years in software and the last 9 building cloud data platforms. I spent seven years on the team that built Adevinta Spain's multi-tenant data mesh platform, which processes more than 38 million events per hour, where I designed the source data contracts and moved the existing tables to data products without disrupting consumers. Today I shape the Azure Databricks lakehouse behind a loyalty product for a European pharma group: contract-driven ingestion, Lakeflow declarative pipelines, observability and data quality.

I am at my best where architecture meets people: turning business needs into contracts, standards and reusable platform patterns, and helping product squads adopt them. I also teach data engineering in master's programmes and in public.

Core strengths

  • Platform architecture: multi-tenant lakehouse and data mesh platforms, data products, source-aligned data contracts, medallion design, self-service ingestion.
  • Governance and compliance: GDPR by design, automated detection of sensitive fields, schema evolution, data quality expectations, observability.
  • Technical direction: engineering standards (Terraform, Databricks), contract definition with product squads, client-facing delivery, hiring interviews.
  • Teaching and communication: course author, university course materials, public talks and livestreams.
